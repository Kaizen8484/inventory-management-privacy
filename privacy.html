Inventory Management

Privacy policy

Last updated 23 November 2026

When you sign in

Inventory Management uses Auth0 to sign you in. If you choose Facebook, Facebook handles the sign-in and shares the profile information you authorize with Auth0. The app uses your Auth0 user identifier to find your inventory account. On your first visit, it checks your email address and its verification status before linking or creating your account.

What the app stores and why

The app stores your account identifier, email address, Auth0 identifier, display name, and access role in MongoDB Atlas. A new account starts with the name “Anonymous User.” You can update your name and provide a phone number in your profile. The app uses these details to display your profile, and your role determines which features you can access. Account administrators can manage account records.

The app also stores the inventory, maintenance, and license information you enter so authorized users can manage those records. Your browser keeps a local copy of your account ID, display name, email address, and role. Signing out clears that copy. Your account record persists across visits.

Privacy questions and account deletion

To request access, a correction, or deletion of your account data, contact the administrator of your Inventory Management app. Identify the email address you used to sign in and describe your request. Please keep passwords and access tokens private.
